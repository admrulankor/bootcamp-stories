# Capitalization of "You" in a license

Capitalizing "You" in a license can feel similar to traditions that capitalize pronouns for deities. To avoid unintended connotations, apply one of these consistent approaches:

- Use lowercase and plain language
    - Example: "you" means the person or entity using the software.
    - Benefit: clear, non‑formal tone; less risk of implying reverence.

- Define a capitalized term explicitly
    - Add a Definitions section: `"You" means the licensee or authorized user.`
    - Then use "You" consistently as a defined legal term.
    - Benefit: precise legal meaning when necessary.

Recommendation: prefer lowercase "you" for readability unless legal precision requires a defined, capitalized term.
